OLADIPUPO ABRAHAM AYODELE (SENIOR PARTNER)

Oladipupo Abraham Ayodele, is an Advocate and Solicitor of the Supreme Court of Nigeria with over 16 years of practice experience. He attended Olabisi Onabanjo University, Ago Iwoye, Ogun State, Nigeria where he obtained his LLB and also the Nigeria Law School for his BL. He recently concluded his Masters in Law (LLM) from Babcock University, Ogun State His core areas of practice include appeals, civil litigation, criminal litigation, election petition, property law, commercial transactions, arbitration, insolvency, corporate finance and family law and matrimonial matters. He has represented government institutions including the Independent National Electoral Commission in litigation matters; multinational companies in the Oil and Gas Sector; Food and Beverage; Fintech, Property and Corporate Finance. Abraham Oladipupo is a firm believer in using his practice and professional knowledge to advance the course of humanity, hence he has rendered services to indigent people pro bono especially in matters relating to human rights and criminal matters. Abraham Oladipupo is associated with the following professional bodies: Nigeria Bar Association (NBA); Business Recovery and Insolvency Practitioners Association of Nigeria (BRIPAN); The Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN) and the Association of Professional Negotiators and Mediators (APNM). He is a Fellow of Global Lawyers Fellowship (GLFP) and also a Fellow of the Institute of Management Consultants, Nigeria. Abraham Oladipupo has contributed to international journals such as Getting the Deal Through (now Panoramic); World Justice Project and the African Journal of Legal Research.

OLUWATOSIN SOLANKE (SENIOR ASSOCIATE)

Oluwatosin Solanke holds an LL.B from Olabisi Onabanjo University and a Master’s Degree in Commercial Law from the University of Cape Town, South Africa. She brings on board over 14 years of extensive experience, in which she has distinguished herself as a trusted legal advisor in corporate commercial law, property law, international commercial transactions, negotiation, and intellectual property. Oluwatosin advises on the structuring, drafting, and review of transaction documents for complex and high-value domestic and cross-border deals, ensuring compliance, mitigating risks, and supporting high-net-worth individuals and organizations in achieving their commercial objectives. She routinely provides transactional and general legal advisory services across diverse sectors and industries. A versatile lawyer, she combines strong litigation skills with deep transactional expertise. As a litigator, Oluwatosin represents high-profile clients before Nigerian courts at all levels. As a corporate lawyer, she delivers comprehensive legal services to both individuals and corporations, offering commercially focused advice that enables clients to successfully navigate operational and regulatory challenges. Her other areas of specialization include contract drafting, negotiation of business agreements, estate planning, alternative dispute resolution, and comprehensive support for intellectual property protection and enforcement.

PRECIOUS TITUS (ASSOCIATE)

Precious Titus is an Associate at Emollient Law Practice, where she advises on matters spanning Corporate Finance, Corporate and Commercial Law, Property Law, Litigation and Intellectual Property. Her practice is centred on providing commercially informed legal advice, developing effective legal strategies and assisting clients in navigating complex transactions, regulatory considerations and disputes. She has a particular interest in the intersection of law, finance and business, with a focus on the legal frameworks underpinning commercial transactions, investment structures, financing arrangements and the protection of business interests. Precious has a strong interest in corporate finance and commercial transactions, including debt financing, investment structuring, transaction documentation, due diligence and the allocation of commercial risk. Her property practice encompasses real estate transactions, property acquisition, title investigation, development arrangements and property-related disputes. She also undertakes litigation and dispute resolution work, with experience in legal research, case preparation, drafting court processes and developing litigation strategies. Her intellectual property interests extend to technology, innovation and the evolving legal considerations surrounding artificial intelligence, digital assets and data protection. She is particularly interested in how emerging technologies and new business models are shaping commercial relationships and the development of legal frameworks. Precious obtained her LL.B. from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, graduating among the top five per cent of her class. She completed her professional legal training at the Nigerian Law School and was subsequently called to the Nigerian Bar.

OSENI QUEEN ELEOJO (PRACTICE MANAGER)

Oseni Queen Eleojo is the Practice Manager at Emollient Law Practice, bringing six years of experience in the firm's administrative and operational management. She holds a degree in Political Science from the National Open University of Nigeria. In her capacity as Practice Manager, she oversees the firm's administrative operations, coordinates personnel and manages the day-to-day activities essential to the effective and efficient running of the practice. Her role involves ensuring operational efficiency, fostering effective internal coordination and supporting the firm's commitment to excellence in service delivery. Over the years, Queen has developed strong leadership, organisational, communication and problem-solving skills, which she applies with professionalism, discipline and adaptability. She is recognised for her composure, resourcefulness and ability to manage responsibilities effectively, contributing significantly to the firm's organisational effectiveness and overall success. Her dedication to maintaining a well-structured, responsive and professionally managed practice makes her an invaluable part of the Emollient Law Practice team.
